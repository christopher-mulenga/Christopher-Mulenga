## Who I Am

I'm Christopher — a founder, ICT educator, and network technology student based in Lusaka, Zambia. In January 2024 I registered **Otrava Technologies**, a Zambian ICT company focused on accessible and affordable digital solutions, with particular attention to assistive technology for visually impaired learners.

Most days I'm doing one of three things: building something, teaching someone, or figuring out why a piece of software is not doing what I told it to.

- 🏢 **Founder & Director** at [Otrava Technologies](https://otravatechnologies.site/) — registered under PACRA, based at Libala Stage 3, Lusaka
- 🎓 **B.ICT (Network Technology)** student at Information and Communications University, Zambia *(Jan 2026–present)*
- 🖥️ **ICT Educator** — I teach visually impaired and blind learners using JAWS screen reader technology
- 📚 **Background:** Secondary Teacher's Diploma in Computer Science with Mathematics — Evelyn Hone College / University of Zambia *(2018–2020)*
