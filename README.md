### 🚀 About Me

ICT enthusiast, builder, and lifelong learner turning ideas into practical digital experiences. I enjoy creating, teaching, experimenting with technology, and finding smarter ways to solve everyday problems.

🔭 &nbsp;I'm currently working on **Building digital solutions through Otrava Technologies, while exploring web development, automation, AI, and practical ICT systems.**  
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently learning **Deepening my knowledge in software development, AI, databases, networking, and modern web technologies — always learning something new.**  
👯 &nbsp;I'm looking to collaborate on **Interesting projects involving web development, AI, education, assistive technology, automation, and digital solutions that solve real problems.**  
🤔 &nbsp;I'm looking for help with **Exploring better ways to turn ambitious ideas into scalable, useful, and beautifully simple technology.**  
💬 &nbsp;Ask me about **ICT, web development, computers, networking, technology education, assistive technology, troubleshooting, and turning “what if?” into “let's build it.”**  
😄 &nbsp;Pronouns: **He / Him**  
⚡ &nbsp;Fun fact: **I can turn a random idea into a project before I have completely figured out how I'm going to build it.**
